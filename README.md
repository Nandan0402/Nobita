# 🏡 Nobita’s House

## 📌 Overview
This project describes **Nobita’s house**, a **small, peaceful two-story home** known for its simple and calm design.  
The house reflects a warm, family-friendly atmosphere with natural colors and greenery.

---

## 🏠 House Description
- 🧱 **Walls:** Cream-colored exterior  
- 🟥 **Roof:** Red, sloped roofing  
- 🪟 **Windows:** Large sliding windows with wooden shutters  
- 🌿 **Surroundings:** Neatly grown green bushes around the house  
- 🚪 **Entrance:** Wooden door with a glass window at the front  

---

## ✨ Key Highlights
- 🏡 Simple and peaceful residential design  
- 🌱 Natural surroundings enhance calmness  
- 🎨 Soft colors give a warm, homely feel  
- 🪟 Spacious windows for light and ventilation
